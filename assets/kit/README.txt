AI FRIENDLY — BADGE KIT

A free mark for the AI-positive web.
Open to AI. Accountable to humans.

WHICH BADGE
-----------
lockup-*      The default horizontal lockup. Ink for light surfaces, cream for dark.
seal-*        Square seal for posters, packaging and press pages.
built-*       AI helped make the work. A disclosure, not a quality claim.
open-*        A lighter statement of intent.
icon-*        Square mark for avatars, favicons and small spaces.
verified-*    For reviewed services only. Do not self-assign.
mark.svg      The mark alone, transparent, for light backgrounds.
mark-cream.svg  The mark alone, transparent, for dark backgrounds.

THE MARK
--------
The mark is drawn on a fixed 360-unit artboard: a concave four-point spark,
one ribbon orbit and one ember dot (15.7-unit radius). The shapes are fixed
and must not be redrawn by hand.

PALETTES
--------
lockup, seal, statement, verified: spectrum ribbon. Use the light files on
paper and the dark files on ink; the white stroke is part of the mark.
icon-*: monochrome, for small sizes where the spectrum ribbon would be muddy.
mark.svg / mark-cream.svg: the spectrum mark alone, for light and dark.

If a surface needs a different palette, change fills only. Keep the geometry,
the 2-unit stroke and the dot. Do not invent a new shape.
Use the supplied vector files.

USING THEM
----------
- Use the supplied files. Do not redraw or distort the mark. Keep the geometry.
- Keep at least one eighth of the mark height clear on every side.
- Minimum width: 120 px for the wordmark, 24 px for the icon.
- Do not imply that AI Friendly reviewed or endorsed you.
- Do not use the verified badge without a current listing.
- The mark is not a content licence. Your terms still govern your work.
